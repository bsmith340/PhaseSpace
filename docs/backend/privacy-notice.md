## The short version

You can use BCS Academy without an account. If you make one, we keep the smallest amount of information we need to save
your progress between devices. We never sell it, never show adverts and never use it to profile you.

## Who we are

BCS Academy is run by [Ben Smith, address or school if appropriate]. We are the "controller" of your data. Contact:
[contact email].

## What we store and why

| What | Why | Lawful basis |
|---|---|---|
| Your email address and password (the password is stored scrambled; we can't read it) | So you can sign in | Contract: providing the service you asked for |
| Display name (a nickname is fine), year group, subjects | To show your name on your account page and suggest the right units | Contract |
| School or college, only if you choose to add it | To understand which schools use the site | Consent (you can remove it any time) |
| Your progress: chapters read, drill scores, saved questions and notes, paper marks, settings | To keep your progress in sync between your devices | Contract |
| Problem reports you send: message, the page, your browser type, your email only if you give it | To fix mistakes on the site and, if you asked, reply to you | Legitimate interests |
| Whether you want occasional emails about new features (off unless you tick it) | Only if you opt in | Consent |
| When you agreed to this notice | To show you were told what we keep | Legal obligation |

We **don't** ask for or store your date of birth, phone number, home address, photo or location. We don't use tracking
cookies or analytics on your account. The site stores your progress in your own browser; that stays on your device unless
you sign in.

## Who else sees it

- **Supabase** (our database and sign-in provider) stores it for us on servers in **London, UK**, under a data processing
  agreement.
- **[Email provider, e.g. Resend]** sends sign-in and password-reset emails.
- Nobody else, unless the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

- Your account and progress: until you delete your account. Accounts not used for **2 years** may be deleted after we
  email you a warning.
- Problem reports: deleted **12 months** after they are closed. If you delete your account, reports you sent are kept
  without your name or email so the fix isn't lost.
- Backups: kept for **30 days**, encrypted, then deleted automatically. Deleted accounts disappear from backups within 30 days.

## Your rights

You can, at any time:

- **See and download** everything we hold about you: *Account → Download my data*.
- **Correct** it: *Account → Profile*.
- **Delete** your account: *Account → Delete account*. It is removed straight away.
- **Stop** marketing emails: untick the box on your Account page, or use the link in any email.
- **Ask us** anything about your data, or to object or restrict how we use it: [contact email]. We reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first. You can also complain to the
**Information Commissioner's Office**: ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113.

## If you're under 18

This site is designed for sixth-form students. You can make an account yourself; we only collect what's needed to run
it, high-privacy settings are the default, and there's nothing public: your profile and progress are visible only to you.
If you're worried about anything, talk to a parent, carer or teacher, or contact us.

## Changes

If we change this notice in a way that matters, we'll tell you when you next sign in. The version date is at the top.
